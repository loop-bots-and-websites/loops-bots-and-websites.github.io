Domain Recon

Passive checks on a domain you own or are allowed to assess: DNS records, SPF and DMARC, HTTPS security headers, certificate expiry and subdomains from public certificate logs.

$ python domain_recon.py example.com

  • Python
  • OSINT
  • DNS

Email Finder

Generates the most likely business email formats for a person at a company domain and checks that the domain can receive mail. Works one person at a time or from a CSV.

  • Outreach
  • MX lookup
